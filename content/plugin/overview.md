# Already on WordPress?

Keep WordPress as the editor. **CF Press Sync** copies posts, media, categories and comments to Cloudflare, and readers are served from the edge — no PHP, no database in the request path.

[Download 2.2.1](/releases/plugin/latest.zip) · 130 KB · WordPress 5.5+, PHP 7.0+ · [GitHub](https://github.com/yuebinliu/cf-press-sync)

## Two things it does not do

**It does not move your content out of WordPress.** WordPress stays the source of truth; the plugin mirrors it. Uninstall, and your WordPress site is exactly as it was.

**It does not bring your theme.** The static site is rendered by a CF Press theme — five ship in the box. Plan on restyling, or keep both sites live while you tune and switch DNS last.
